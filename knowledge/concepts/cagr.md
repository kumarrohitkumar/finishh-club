# CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)
CAGR is the average yearly growth rate of an investment.

If 100 grows to 200 over five years, that is 100% total growth, but 14.87% per
year. CAGR is the second number.

It is useful because it lets you compare investments held for different lengths
of time. Total growth cannot do that - 50% over two years and 50% over ten years
are very different things.

CAGR smooths out the journey. It does not tell you how bumpy the ride was.
