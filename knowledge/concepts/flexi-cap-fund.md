# Flexi Cap fund
A Flexi Cap fund invests in company shares of any size - large, mid or small -
with no fixed split between them.

The fund manager decides how much goes where and can change it at any time. If
they think smaller companies look better, they can move more money there.

SEBI requires at least 65% of the fund to be in shares. Beyond that, the manager
is free.

It is the most common equity category in India, and the least restricted.
