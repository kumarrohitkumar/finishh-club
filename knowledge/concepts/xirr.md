# XIRR
XIRR is the return rate when you invest at different times rather than all at
once.

CAGR assumes one investment on one date. That does not describe a SIP, where you
put in money every month. Each instalment has been invested for a different
length of time.

XIRR accounts for the date and size of every instalment and produces one yearly
rate. It is the correct way to measure SIP returns.
