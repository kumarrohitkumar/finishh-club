# Multi Cap fund
A Multi Cap fund also invests across company sizes, but unlike a Flexi Cap fund
it has a fixed minimum in each.

SEBI requires at least 25% each in large cap, mid cap and small cap - 75% in
total - with the remaining 25% wherever the manager chooses.

This is the difference people most often get wrong:

  Flexi Cap  - manager can put everything in large caps if they want
  Multi Cap  - must hold at least 25% in mid and small caps, always

So a Multi Cap fund always carries some small-company exposure. A Flexi Cap
fund may carry none.
