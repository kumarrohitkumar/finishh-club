# SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)
A SIP means investing a fixed amount on a fixed date every month, instead of
one large amount at once.

Because the amount is fixed, you buy more units when the NAV is low and fewer
when it is high. Over time this averages your purchase price.

A SIP does not guarantee profit and does not protect against losses in a falling
market. What it does is remove the need to decide when to invest, which is a
decision most people get wrong.
