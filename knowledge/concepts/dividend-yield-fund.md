# Dividend Yield fund
A Dividend Yield fund invests mainly in companies that pay out a good share of
their profits as dividends.

SEBI requires at least 65% of the fund to be in shares, chosen for their
dividend yield.

Such companies are often older and steadier - utilities, banks, established
manufacturers. The fund itself does not have to pay you a dividend; the name
describes what it invests in, not what it pays out.
