# Arbitrage fund
An Arbitrage fund makes money from small price differences between the same
share in two markets - the cash market and the futures market.

It buys in one and sells in the other at the same time, locking in the gap. The
gap is small, so returns are modest and usually resemble a short-term debt fund
rather than an equity fund.

It is classified as equity for tax purposes even though it behaves defensively.
