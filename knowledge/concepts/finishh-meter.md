# The Finishh Meter
The Finishh Meter shows how often a fund produced each kind of outcome in the
past, over a holding period you choose.

It takes every possible window of that length in the fund's history, moving one
day at a time, works out the return per year for each window, and sorts them
into four groups: strong, moderate, flat, and loss.

It answers "how often did this happen before". It does not predict what will
happen next, and it does not describe what real investors earned.

Each type of fund is judged on its own scale, because a debt fund and an equity
fund are different instruments. The scale in use is always shown on the meter.
