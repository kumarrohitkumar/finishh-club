# Risk level (riskometer)
SEBI requires every mutual fund to display a risk level: Low, Low to Moderate,
Moderate, Moderately High, High, or Very High.

It is set by rules, not opinion, based on what the fund holds.

A higher risk level is not a warning to stay away. It describes how much the
value can move. An equity fund is expected to be High or Very High - that is
normal for equity, not a fault.
