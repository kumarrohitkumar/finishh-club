# NAV (Net Asset Value)
NAV is the price of one unit of a mutual fund.

It is worked out once at the end of each working day: take everything the fund
owns, subtract what it owes, and divide by the number of units investors hold.

A fund with a NAV of 45 is not "cheaper" than one with a NAV of 450. The number
on its own tells you nothing about whether a fund is good. What matters is how
much the NAV has grown over time, not what it happens to be today.

NAV is published by AMFI every working day. It does not move during the day the
way a share price does.
