# Tracking error
Tracking error measures how closely an index fund follows the index it copies.

A fund tracking the Nifty 50 should return whatever the Nifty 50 returns, minus
its fee. In practice it drifts slightly, because of cash held for redemptions,
trading costs and timing.

Lower tracking error means the fund copies the index more faithfully. For an
index fund, that is the whole job.
