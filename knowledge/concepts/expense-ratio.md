# Expense ratio
The expense ratio is the yearly fee the fund company charges to run the fund.

It is shown as a percentage of your money. An expense ratio of 1.5% means about
1.5% of your investment goes to the fund company each year.

You never see this charged separately. It is already taken out before the NAV is
published, so the returns you see are after the fee.

It matters more than it looks. Over twenty years, the difference between 0.5%
and 1.5% a year is a large amount of money, because you lose both the fee and
the growth that money would have produced.
