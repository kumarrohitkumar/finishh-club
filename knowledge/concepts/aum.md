# AUM (Assets Under Management)
AUM is the total amount of money the fund is managing.

A large AUM means many investors trust the fund, and usually means better
liquidity. A very large AUM can make a fund harder to manage in small company
stocks, because buying or selling moves the price.

AUM on its own does not tell you whether a fund is good. A small fund is not
automatically risky and a large one is not automatically safe.
