# Direct plan vs Regular plan
The same fund is sold in two versions.

A Regular plan pays a commission to whoever sold it to you - a distributor,
bank or agent. That commission is inside the expense ratio.

A Direct plan has no distributor, so the expense ratio is lower, usually by
0.5% to 1% a year.

Everything else is identical: same fund manager, same holdings, same strategy.
The only difference is the fee, and therefore the returns.
