# Sectoral or Thematic fund
A Sectoral fund invests in one industry - banking, pharma, technology. A
Thematic fund invests around one idea that spans industries, such as
manufacturing or consumption.

SEBI requires at least 80% of the fund to follow that sector or theme.

Because everything in the fund depends on the same thing, these funds move
together. When the sector does badly, there is nothing else in the fund to
soften it. They are the least diversified equity category.
